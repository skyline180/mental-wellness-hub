Your website has the following main sections:

Introduction Section: Welcomes users with a call-to-action button.

Books Section: Showcases recommended books on mental health.
About Section: Provides information about the website and its mission.
Services Section: Describes the mental health services offered.
Contact Section: Includes a form for users to get in touch.
